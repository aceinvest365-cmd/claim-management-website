What We Do

Everything you need to manage claims end-to-end

From first notice of loss to final settlement, ClaimTrack provides the tools to keep every claim on track.

Log Claims Digitally

Submit new claims through a guided digital form — no paperwork, no confusion. All information captured upfront for faster processing.

Upload Documents & Photos

Drag and drop supporting evidence — photos, invoices, police reports — directly onto the claim. Everything stored securely in one place.

Track Claim Progress Live

See exactly where a claim is in the pipeline — from lodged to assessed, approved, and paid — with real-time status updates.

Automatic Notifications

Get instant alerts when a claim is updated, a document is requested, or an approval comes through. Never miss a beat.

Centralized Communication

Keep all messages, notes, and queries in one thread per claim. No more lost emails or scattered conversations across channels.

Digital Audit Trail

Every action logged. Every change timestamped. A complete, tamper-proof record for compliance, governance, and dispute resolution.

Driver Management & Live Tracking

Monitor your fleet in real time. Get instant alerts for area breaches, standstill detection, and hours-worked thresholds — all from one dashboard.

High Risk Area Mapping

Visualize incident hotspots across South Africa with interactive heatmaps. Identify patterns and deploy resources where they matter most.

Shipment Tracker

Track active deliveries in real time with transit status updates. Know exactly where your goods are at every stage of the journey.

AI-Powered Intelligence

Leverage artificial intelligence for automated claim analysis, smart recommendations, fraud detection hints, and predictive risk scoring.

Data Import & Export

Import and export claims and policies in CSV, JSON, or Excel format. Migrate data effortlessly and generate custom reports on demand.

PDF Report Generation

Export any claim as a comprehensive PDF report — complete with status, policy details, incident info, and supporting documentation.

API Connectivity

Connect ClaimTrack to your existing systems with RESTful APIs. Seamless data exchange with insurers, brokers, and third-party platforms.

Google Maps Integration

Pin incident locations with address autocomplete, view claims on interactive maps, and analyze geographic trends across your portfolio.
